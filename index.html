Questions

How long does a suit take?

Most custom suits take 4-6 weeks to complete, depending on the design, fabric availability, and fitting requirements. If you have a specific deadline, please let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate your schedule.

Do I pay a deposit?

Yes. We require a 50% deposit before work begins. This allows us to purchase materials and reserve production time. The remaining balance is due upon completion of your garment.

Can you work from my photo?

Absolutely. You can send us photos, inspiration images, or screenshots of a design you like. We'll use them as a reference and work with you to create a garment tailored to your measurements and preferences.

What if I am not happy with the fit?

You approve the fit at the final fitting. If something is not right, we adjust it until it is.

Do you deliver?

We pickup and deliver within our service area. Delivery terms would be discussed during the consultation.

Can I bring my own fabric?

Yes. We will tell you honestly whether it suits the style you want.
